# Restoration boundaries

Exact bytes do not prove an executable restore. Public source/reference bindings preserve provenance; raw SkullBreak point/reference NPZ, historical SPCG predictions, initial U/H bytes, provider/CUDA binaries and four trained parent weights are local-only and must be restored independently. Do not download/upgrade dependencies implicitly. Prior requests/control files are records, not authorization. No inference, checkpoint deserialization or restore execution has been performed during archival.
